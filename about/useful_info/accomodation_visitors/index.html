Accomodation for visitors

Hotels and Residences

If you are visiting us you will probably be staying at the college accommodation at:

Aleu Residence
Address: Carrer de Pau Gargallo, 30 Barcelona 08028
Phone: +34 93 144 01 40
E-mail: aleu@nexoresidencias.com
Metro: Palau Reial (Line 3, Green)
Web: https://nexoresidencias.com/en/aleu-university-residence
Map: Here

How to get there   [+]

Aleu Residence is within walking distance to the Faculty of Physics. You can follow the directions given in "How to get here" above to get to PALAU REIAL metro estation. From the PALAU REIAL metro station exit, go down the perpendicular street (C. Martí i Franqués) one block. Then turn right (C. Pau Gargallo) and the residence is right there.

See graphic instructions in Google Map).

Residència Torre Girona
Address: Passeig dels Til.lers 19      08028 Barcelona
Phone: +34 93 3904300
E-mail: torregirona@resa.es
Metro: Palau Reial (Line 3, Green)
Web: http://www.resa.es/esl/residencias/torre_girona
Map: Here

How to get there   [+]

Torre Girona Residence Hall is also within walking distance to the Faculty of Physics. You can follow the directions given in "How to get here" above to get to PALAU REIAL metro estation. From the PALAU REIAL metro station exit, cross Av. Diagonal (the large avenue where the Faculty of Physics is) and go up the perpendicular street (C. Tinent Coronel Valenzuela) until the end. Then turn right (C. Jordi Girona) and follow the street until the end, and turn left (Passeig dels Tilers). The residence is at a few meters distance.

See graphic instructions in Google Map).

Residència d´Investigadors CSIC
Address: C/ Hospital  64       08001 Barcelona
Ph.: +34 934438610

E-mail: investigadors@resa.es
Metro: Liceu (Line 3, Green), Sant Antoni (Line 2, Purple)

How to get there   [+]

This residence is in the city center. To get there from the airport, take the train or bus to Pl. Catalunya. Once there, take Metro Line 3 to Liceu (one stop). Close to the station exit , on the left sidewalk of the Rambla (going uphill on the Rambla from the sea to Pl. Catalunya), you will find the street Carrer de l'Hospital, in which the residence is located. If you are travelling light you might prefer to walk down the Rambla from Pl. Catalunya until you come across (to your right) Carrer de l'Hospital. Click here for further public transportation instructions.

The CSIC residence and the Faculty of Physics are connected via metro Line 3, stops Liceu and Palau Reial, resp.Click here for further public transportation instructions.
