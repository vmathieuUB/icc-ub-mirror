STEPS TO SIGNING A CONTRACT

If you are to sign a contract or grant to work at the UB, you will be required several documents. We present some useful information about the processes you will have to follow to obtain these documentation.

IMPORTANT:The information given here is just illustrative. The documentation required and the processes to obtain might change at any moment, and it might be different depending on the kind of contract, nationality, or personal situation of the candidate. Please:

  • Contact the person in charge with your contract and ask him/her for an exact description of what you have to do.
  • Always check official information for last updates.

Last update: 25 July 2016

Offices in charge

Please ask to your contact researcher at ICCUB which unit is in charge with your contract, and contact them as soon as possible. If you are going to work at the ICCUB, you will probably sign your contract with one of this units:

Documentation

We list here some documentation you will probably be required if you are coming to work at ICCUB, and illustrative description of the processes to get it. Please contact the person in charge with your contract for definite information, and check the official sites for policy changes.

  • VISA    [+]

    Required to: People who are not national of any European Union country or Liechtenstein, Iceland, Norway and Switzerland.

    Where: Information about Embassies and Consulates


  • Card/Number Identification as a Foreigner (TIE/NIE)    [+]

    TIE is required for: People who are not national of any European Union country or Liechtenstein, Iceland, Norway and Switzerland.

    NIE is required for: People who are national of any European Union country or Liechtenstein, Iceland, Norway and Switzerland.

    Official information: TIE    NIE

    To get a NIE in Barcelona it is mandatory to make an appointment: Cita previa.

    You can ask for an appointment from your country.

    IMPORTANT: Due to the large number of requests, they might be able to issue appointments only for 3 weeks later, so you should do this 1 month in advance. Do not wait until the last moment to ask for an appointment!


    NOTE: If the online system does not allow for new appointments, this means that the maximum number of appointments has been is reached. Please try another day.

    If 15 days before the starting date of your contract the problem persists, you should contact the person in charge with your contract.

    Address

    On the date and time of the appointment you have to go to the police station:

    Jefatura Superior de Policía de Cataluña
    Rambla de Guipúscoa, 76-80, 08020 Barcelona
    Google Maps

    Required documentation

    • Application Form (TIE: EX17    NIE: EX18)

      • IMPORTANT: Do not mark "CONSIENTO que las comunicaciones y notificaciones se realicen por medios electrónicos". If you do, inform yourself of the possible repercussions.

    • Passport or ID card (ORIGINAL and COPY)
    • Work Certificate (ORIGINAL)  [+]

      This certificate should be issued by the person in charge with your contract. Please contact the person in charge of your contract (see above).

      If you wish, you can ask the person in charge with the certificate to send it to an ICCUB researcher or to the ICCUB Secretariat [ICCUB Address]
