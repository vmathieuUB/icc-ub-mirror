REACH THE ICCUB

ADDRESS
Institut de Ciències del Cosmos
Facultat de Física
Martí i Franquès, 1
E-08028 Barcelona
PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION
Metro: Line 3 (green), "Palau Reial" stop
Bus: 7, 33, 60, 67, 75, 113, H6
Tram: Lines T1, T2, T3

The most convenient way to reach the ICCUB is to take Metro Line 3 (GREEN), towards ZONA UNIVERSITARIA and exit at the second to last station, called PALAU REIAL:  the Physics and Chemistry Faculties are in the building across the exit. Once inside the building, follow the directions explained in Researcher's Offices / ICCUB Secretariat .

We recomend you to buy the T-10 card, which can be used for most types of public transportation in the area of Barcelona. Note that getting to/from the airport by metro requires to pay a supplement fare.

How to reach the ICCUB from...
THE AIRPORT    [+]

BY METRO: The subway or "metro" connects both terminals of the Airport with Barcelona underground network since February 2016. The line is called L9 Sud. There are stops at T1 (inside the building) and T2 (outside the building, next to train station), in both cases follow the signs. You can take L9 until the metro towards ZONA UNIVERSITARIA and change to Line 3 (GREEN). Once in Line 3 (GREEN), get off in PALAU REIAL. Check this map of Barcelona city public transport. The bus routes are not included in this map.

BY BUS: There is a bus as well (Aerobus) which takes you downtown from the airport. You will find the bus stop as you leave any of the airport terminals. It runs every 5 min, approx and the price of a single ticket is 5,9 Eur. Take the bus to PLAÇA D'ESPANYA (first stop in town). Once there, look for the metro station; take Line 3 (GREEN) towards ZONA UNIVERSITARIA and get off in PALAU REIAL.

BY TRAIN: Alternatively, there is a train (Renfe) every 30 min which takes you to Sants Central Station and to downtown. The train station at the airport is close to Terminal 2 (T2, lowcost terminal), there is a footbridge. From Terminal 1 (T1) there is a shuttle bus connecting to T2 (then you need to cross the footbridge). Terminal C (Barcelona-Madrid shuttle) is part of the T2. You want to exit at SANTS ESTACIO. Once there look for the Metro Station, Line 3 (GREEN) towards ZONA UNIVERSITARIA and get off in PALAU REIAL.

BY TAXI: If you decide to take a taxi, this may cost around 25 euros, but the price varies depending on traffic.

More useful info about the airport and about travelling from the airport.

SANTS TRAIN STATION   [+]

Go to the main hall of the station and look for Metro Line 3 (GREEN). Now follow instructions given above

Hints:

- Right by the entrance to the Metro there is an information booth where you can get a free plan of the metro line.

- If you're carrying heavy luggage, you should be aware that it's a fairly long walk from the hall of Sants Station to the platform for line 3, through underground corridors with several stairways and often without lifts nor escalators.

Timetable of Renfe trains.  More detailed and useful info on Sants station.

BY CAR   [+]

The ICCUB is on Diagonal Avenue, in the upper part of the city, at the right-hand-side if you drive towards the city center. From Diagonal Avenue, driving towards the city center, turn right in Martí i Franquès and you are there. There is a big parking lot (Parc de Bederrida, big empty square in photo), free of charge, behind the Faculty of Physics and Chemistry building.

Beware: you should never, ever, try to come by car if there's a football match at the nearby stadium of FC Barcelona.

To look for more detailed instructions: Google Maps
