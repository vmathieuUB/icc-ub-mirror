Researchers' Offices

Researchers of the ICCUB have their offices in:

  1. Physics Faculty: PHYS1, PHYS2
  2. Chemistry Faculty: CHEM
  3. Mathematics Faculty: MATH

Please visit the directory to find the office number of location of any ICCUB member.

PHYSICS FACULTY

Entrances

PHYS1

PHYS2 and CHEM

The Physics departments are in the same building as the ICCUB Secretariat.

The main entrance is in the small street Marti i Franquès, which is perpendicular to the large Diagonal Avenue. See map.

PHYS1

For the Physics Faculty offices, once you enter the building through Martí i Franquès entrance, you should turn left and walk through a long corridor until the end of it. Then, you will see the two elevators of the old wing of the building. Take them to go to the floors corresponding to the ICCUB. See map.

The first number of the office tells you the floor where it is located. Once in that floor, if you have taken the old elevators, you will arrive to a hall where there are two big maps telling you the location of the office.

PHYS2

Offices with numbers ending with "I" also belong to the Physics Faculty, but it is easier to get there trough the Chemistry Faculty. They are in the 7th floor.

Once you enter the building through Martí i Franquès entrance, you should turn right and take the elevators that you will see at the corner (see map). Go to the 7th floor and once there, turn right and walk through a long corridor. Just before the emergency exit, you will see a glass door at your right with the sign "Institut de Ciències del Cosmos". Cross the door to the offices area.

CHEMISTRY FACULTY

CHEM

The Chemistry departmens are also in the same building as well as it is the ICCUB Secretariat. The main entrance is in the small street Marti i Franquès, which is perpendicular to the large Diagonal Avenue.

For the Chemistry Faculty offices, once you enter the building through Martí i Franquès entrance, you should turn right and take the elevators that you will see at the corner.

The first number of the office tells you the floor where it is located.

MATHEMATICS FACULTY

MATH

The Mathematics Faculty is in the city center:

Facultat de Matemàtiques
Departament de Matemàtica Aplicada
Edifici Històric
Gran Via de les Corts Catalanes, 585
08007 Barcelona
Ph: +34 93 402 16 50

To get to the offices of ICCUB members at Mathematics Faculty (Departament of Applied Mathematics) the best way is to enter the building through the left wing entrance, located in Aribau street. Cross the new building and once in the garden, turn left and walk up to a provisional white building. The offices are in that building.
