Meeting point for radiation and photon
detectors community

Following the third Barcelona Techno week (course on semiconductor detectors), we are also organizing a meeting point for professionals working on instrumentation with radiation detectors, light detectors and CMOS imagers and their applications. It is open to any Techno Week participant.

We would like to invite as well professionals working in Catalonia in the field of instrumentation for radiation detectors even if they don’t participate in the regular course .

The objective is that the participants learn the activities developed in other research centers and are able to identify synergies and collaborations. For this reason, we would encourage the participants to present their work in this workshop.

In order to organize the workshop and schedule the presentations, we would like to kindly ask you to register if you would be interested to attend the workshop and present your activities. The workshop will be free of charge but registration will be required.

If you wish to present, we would like to ask you fill in the corresponding fields in the registration form.

The workshop will take place in the ICCUB (Physics Faculty of the University of Barcelona) on the Monday 9th July.

We would also be grateful if you could distribute this message to people that could be interested in participating.
