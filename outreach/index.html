Outreach

The ICCUB outreach team manages two websites devoted to Cosmology, Astrophysics and Particle physics. You can find all of our researcher's talks and outreach events, ongoing and past projects, dissemination news and several outreach materials. Do you want to know, for instance, what The Christian or Western calendar is? Take a look into our outreach websites!

 

Find Astronomy and Cosmology news, activities and events. You can also find several outreach resources and materials such as travelling exhibitions and videos. We'll also keep you updated on the astronomical agenda, live webcast of astronomical events as eclipses and planetary transits!

 

Outreach resources and materials on Cosmology, Particle Physics, Gravitation and Atomic & Nuclear Physics. Find past and upcoming activities, workshops and talks, dissemination news and articles.  You will also find a Project section, that includes the Workshop on Particle Physics.

 

Subscribe to our Newsletter!

Keep updated on our planned activities, highlighted news and discoveries, upcoming astronomical events, and find useful materials and resources through our monthly newsletter

Data protection – Basic information

Responsible

Secretaria General de la Universitat de Barcelona

Purpose

To inform you of the activities organized by the Institute of Cosmos Sciences of the UB, such as news and press releases as well as job offers from the ICCUB.

Legitimation

Consent of the concerned party

Recipients

The University of Barcelona and those responsible for handling data, if such is the case. The UB does not transfer data to third parties, unless under a legal obligation.

Rights

You have the right to access, modify, or delete your data. You can also request a data transfer or a limitation of handling.

Responsable

Secretaria General de la Universitat de Barcelona

Finalitat

Informar-vos sobre les activitats organitzades per l’Institut de Ciències del Cosmos de la UB, sobre notícies i comunicats de premsa de l’ICCUB i sobre ofertes de feina de l’ICCUB

Legitimació

Consentiment de l’afectat

Destinataris

Drets

Accedir a les vostres dades, rectificar-les, suprimir-les, sol·licitar-ne la portabilitat i sol·licitar-ne la limitació dels tractaments

Derecho de información – Información básica

Responsable

Secretaria General de la Universitat de Barcelona

Finalidad
